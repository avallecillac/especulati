# Contract: Library, Saved, tags and search UI

## Library

- **Segments:** `Books | Articles | Saved (N)`. The Saved tab's accessible name is
  "Saved".
- **The Saved list:** newest saved first.
  - **Each row:** the title, site · ~N min, and "Saved <date>" in small caps.
  - **Tapping the row** opens the page in a new tab.
  - **A "More" button** opens a sheet with **Mark as read**, **Details** (opens the
    saved article's screen, `#/article/<id>`) and **Remove**. Each action shows a toast
    with Undo.
  - **Empty state:** a Butterfly vignette, and "Nothing saved yet. Save articles to read
    them later."
- **Search:**
  - A search field (`role="searchbox"`, labelled "Search articles") sits above the
    Articles and Saved lists, and searches both.
  - **Results** are one list. Each row has a "Read" or "Saved" label. A row matched
    through a highlight or note shows that passage, with the match in `<mark>`.
  - **Tag filter:** chips under the field (the most used, plus "All tags…").
    - Selected chips have `aria-pressed="true"`.
    - The filter is AND.
    - **Clear** resets both the text and the chips.
- **Tags list:** under "All tags…", a sheet listing every tag with its count, plus
  **Rename** and **Delete** (with Confirm).

## Article screen

- **Tags row:** under the details, the tags appear as chips, each with a remove button
  ("Remove tag <name>").
  - **"Add tag":** an input with suggestions (a listbox, most used first). Enter or a
    suggestion adds it.
- **A saved article** shows "Saved <date>" and a **Mark as read** primary button.

## Capture screen

- In every mode, after saving: **Add tags**, the same input as on the Article screen.

## Tokens only. Decorative art is `aria-hidden`. The a11y and 320 px scans cover Saved, search and tags.
